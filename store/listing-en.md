# Google Play listing (draft, English)

**App name (max. 30):** Pixel Diorama: World Builder

**Short description (max. 80):** Build a cozy 3D pixel-art miniature world. Offline, no ads.

**Full description:**

Build a living miniature world in a stunning 3D pixel-art style.

Become the Mayor and grow a tiny island into a village, a city, and even a magical land. Every island
is a living diorama with a day-night cycle, dynamic weather, lights that glow at dusk, piling snow,
rain, thunderstorms, fog, stars, and auroras.

• 7 unique islands: Green Village, Pine Forest, Tropical Beach, Snowy Peaks, Desert Oasis, Neon City, Fantasy Isle
• 30+ buildings you can upgrade, repaint, rotate, and demolish, including a landmark for each island
• Villagers with daily routines: they work, eat, relax, chat, and sleep
• Gather wood, stone, food, ore, sand, and crystals; turn them into planks, bricks, glass, tools, and magic essence at the Workshop
• An economy with taxes, production, and a Market where prices rise and fall
• Quests, achievements, daily rewards, and random events (caravans, festivals, meteor showers, treasure chests, lost cats)
• Level up to unlock new buildings, recipes, and islands
• Procedurally generated sound and music: wind, rain, waves, birds, crickets, thunder
• Intuitive touch controls: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, twist with two fingers to rotate
• Autosave, and your villagers keep producing while the game is closed
• 100% offline, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection
